# Pet health records — market & competitive analysis

Step 1 (secondary research) for a pet vaccination / health record product.

Published canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/b8961817-a13e-4efd-8b14-6d71fa595eda

## Files

- `Main.dc.html` — the full written analysis (flowing report artboard)
- `Landscape.dc.html` — competitive positioning map
- `RecordJourney.dc.html` — where the vaccination record breaks down, stage by stage
- `canvas.json` — canvas layout and launch view
- `pet-health-records-analysis.html` — the seeded, publishable canvas (regenerated, not hand-edited)

## Regenerating

Edit the `.dc.html` working files, then re-seed a fresh canvas and republish to the same URL.

## Scope

Desk research only — published market reports, app listings, competitor sites, trade press
and regulator guidance. Market figures are directional; sources disagree by up to 2x.
No primary research has been done yet.
